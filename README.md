# 🌟 Simple Portfolio Website Template  

This is a **clean and minimal portfolio website template** built using **HTML** and **CSS**. It's perfect for individuals looking to showcase their work, skills, and contact information in a simple yet professional manner.  

## 🔹 Features  
✅ **Home Page** – Introduce yourself with a short bio and highlight key skills.  
✅ **Contact Page** – A dedicated section for visitors to connect with you.  
✅ **Fully Responsive** – Works on all screen sizes with a clean layout.  
✅ **Easy to Customize** – Simple HTML & CSS structure for quick modifications.  
✅ **Lightweight & Fast** – No heavy frameworks, just pure HTML & CSS.  

## 🛠️ Technologies Used  
- **HTML5** – Structure of the website.  
- **CSS3** – Styling and layout design.  

## 🎨 How to Customize  
1. **Edit the Content:**  
   - Open **index.html** and replace the placeholder text with your own bio, skills, and details.  
   - Modify **contact.html** with your own contact details or social media links.  

2. **Change the Design:**  
   - Update the styles in **style.css** to match your brand.  
   - Change colors, fonts, and layout as per your preference.  

3. **Add More Pages (Optional):**  
   - Want to showcase projects or blogs? Just create new `.html` files and link them from the navbar.
